### 2a. A re-tally trap, recorded so nobody re-derives a false hole

A naive count of the `drop4` stream gives `88d5` **35,959** distinct subsets,
one short, and the missing one is `drop = [3, 11, 19, 27]` — the unique
`W = 10` set, which is exactly the hardest one in its own histogram
(`{2:360, 3:6372, 4:21799, 5:5376, 6:1755, 7:254, 8:42, 9:1, 10:1}`).
That is **not** a gap: the screen ran with `wmax = 9`, and `RESULT.md` §2 says
so — *"35,959 by the screen …, plus the single `W=10` set — rho-orbit 3 — by
the cube run of §1."* The 35,960th decision is banked in the **`cube`** stream,
121 rows labelled `88d5_orb3_sub` at `k = 9 = W − 1`, all `unsat`.
The remaining 282 surplus `88d5` rows are re-runs, all at `W = 2` (642 rows
against 360 distinct).

`88fs5` needs no such patch: `wmax = 9` is its histogram maximum, and the same
subset `[3, 11, 19, 27]` sits at `W = 9` there and is present in the ledger
(`known_cost: 9, k: 8, secs: 66.2, verdict: unsat`).

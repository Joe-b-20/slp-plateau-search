## 0. Headline

1. **The 498 are internally sound and structurally ordinary.** No duplicate
   mask, no dead gate, `B = 56`, exact `kappa + |counted| + Y` partition and a
   valid maximal-clean-tree structure in **498/498**. They differ from the
   census 88s in *shape of population*, not in kind: they are much **shallower**
   (87 at depth ≤ 6 vs ~4 in an 800-circuit census sample), much less
   **glue-heavy** (median 6 vs 10), and they reach a **lower `kappa` floor**
   (20 vs 24).
2. **No profile-region hits.** The `{4,7}` region of `LEADS.md` §10 needs
   `|A*| = 2`. The minimum over the 498 is **`|A*| = 18`**; the minimum over an
   800-circuit census sample is 20 and over the five published 88s is 20. The
   never-searched region remains never-populated as well as never-searched.
3. **The `l3 = 64` anchors are real but they are not the best anchors known.**
   All 27 rows at `l3 ≤ 66` extract and verify (**27/27 VALID**, banked). But a
   first-ever `l3` sweep of the **e15 Run-A census** — 17,283 88s that have been
   on disk with build orders since e15 — finds **570 at `l3 ≤ 66` and a minimum
   of `l3 = 57`**. The census beats the 498 at every depth but 6. §4.4 reprices
   `atlas_misc.md` L1's "3 gates further into the corner" accordingly.
4. **A new machine-checked negative certificate.** Every single-mask deletion of
   every one of the 498 — **27,888 candidate 87-mask sets, each still containing
   all 32 MixColumns targets** — is **non-realisable as an XOR-SLP**. This is
   strictly stronger than the `B = 56` test that closed the deletion route in
   `CORPUS88.md`. See §7 and `near87.json`.
5. **`l3` and `|A*|` are strongly anti-correlated** (`r = -0.854` over the 498,
   `r = -0.553` over the census sample). The socle-anchoring route (drive `l3`
   down) and the e17 branch-3 route (drive `|A*|` down) pull in **opposite**
   directions. This is a cross-lane fact neither lane could see alone.

Read: **the corrected-ruler walk is a shallow, low-glue, low-`kappa` sampler.**
It reaches parts of the 88 plateau that the campaign-87 population methods

against 0.5 % — and it does so at a systematically *higher* median `l3`. That is
the honest characterisation of what the 498 are: not deeper into the socle
corner, but into a shallower, less glue-dependent quarter of the plateau.

The 283-only subset is not a separate species: on every statistic its spread
contains the 215 shared ones. It is simply *wider* (`kappa` to 35 vs 29, `Y` to
40 vs 30, distance-to-records down to 48 vs 61). Novelty here means "further
out", not "different in kind".

### 3.3 Plane usage and the 8-sector profile

  498 × 88 = 43,824 gates: `dim 1: 14,458 · dim 2: 28,584 · dim 3: 782`.

* **Economy classes** present in all 498: `column`, `diagonal`, `mixed`, `glue`;

  S7 3,476`. S2 carries **1.81×** the traffic of S1. This is a measured

  eight sectors are *"eight copies of one 4-target puzzle"* — the puzzle is
  identical, the realised circuits are not. Recorded, not interpreted.

## 4. The `l3 ≤ 66` anchors (question 2)

### 4.1 Extraction and verification

`ngates a0 b0 a1 b1 …`, index pairs over signals `0..31 = inputs`,


**27/27 VALID MixColumns, 88 gates, 32/32 outputs, depth as recorded.**
Banked as `wrapup/day2/atlas498/anchors/atlas498_l3_<l3>_<canon>.json`, each

### 4.2 What makes the skeletons small

`socle.py:l3_of` reduces a circuit in three ways. Decomposed per anchor
(`anchors_l3le66.json`):

| mechanism | across all 27 anchors |
|---|---|
| gates that are **socle-valued** (`proj = 0`) | **2 or 3** |
| gates whose socle class is **already carried** (class-alias) | **17 – 20** |
| gates dropped by **DCE from the target classes** | 0 – 4 |

The socle-valued count is essentially constant at 2–3 across the entire
`l3 = 64 … 79` range. **All of the variation in `l3` is class-aliasing.** This
settles, quantitatively, the honest self-falsification already recorded in
`third_frame.md` ("glue does not die in the quotient, so *the trick is 3 socle
gates* is wrong"): the trick is not socle gates at all, it is carrying the same
socle class on ~19 different masks. A skeleton-anchored search that budgets
"socle gates" is budgeting the wrong resource.

### 4.3 Two disjoint anchor families

| family | rows | seed | `l3` | depth | `kappa` | glue | overlap w/ records |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`fib_S` / `hunt` from `88@5fs`** | 20 | the from-scratch 88 @ depth 5 | 64–66 | **5–6** | 27–29 | 2–6 | 74–86 |
| **`sh.l70`** | 7 | `work/SH_l70_90.txt`, a 90-gate circuit at `l3 = 70` | 64–66 | 8–10 | **33** | 10–12 | **48–50** |

The two `l3 = 64` circuits belong to *different* families — one sits 86 masks
from a published record, the other 48. **Low `l3` is therefore not a lineage
property**; it is reachable from at least two unrelated starting points. That is
new information the 9-circuit sample on the published socle plane could not
have produced.

## 5. Why they were never analysed (question 3)

Three independent causes, all datable from the session's own files.

**(a) The write-up froze before the data landed.**
`thinktank/ideas_strange_corners.md` — the one write-up covering `corner/` — has
mtime **2026-08-18 10:16**, and says so itself at §7: *"Jobs still running
at the time of writing: `hunt.jobs` (160 walks × 900 CPU-s from 20 distinct
starts), `sh2.jobs` (60 shoulder walks + 16 long descents), `fib.jobs` (32
fibre/skeleton-restricted walks). Their results are not included above and are
not predicted here."* The `.plat` files carrying the 88-gate rows have mtimes
**09:47 → 15:41**. **470 of the 498 sit in files last written after the
write-up's own mtime.** Every job file (`hunt.jobs` 09:39, `sh2.jobs` 09:44,
`fib.jobs` 09:54) was launched in the final hour before the freeze.

**(b) `.plat` was outside the analysis pipeline by construction.**
`corner/envelope.py:36–40` — the atlas's own socle-plane envelope tool — globs
`out/*.txt` and `work/*.BEST.*.txt` and then explicitly `continue`s on any path
containing `".plat"`. `socle.py`, `census.py`, `analyze.py` all read circuits
through `anatomy.load_gates`, which is `json.load`. **No script in `atlas/` can
read a `.plat` row.** The only writer is `walk.c:plat_dump()`, and it fires on
`(ndistinct % 200) == 0` — a sampled *trace*, not a result file. The session's
results were the 707 `.BEST.*` and 548 `.FINAL.*` files; the 320 `.plat` files
were debug output that happened to contain 498 unrecorded 88s.

**(c) The session ended and never came back.** `NOTEBOOK.md`'s
`## SESSION END (2026-08-18, pods terminated by user)` lists four
`NEXT SESSION PICKUP` items — V1.5 corpus completion, basin-sweep restart,
architecture pricing / `C_c` / depth-5 certs, corrected backward engine. Reading
`corner/out/` is not among them, and no successor exists: `"corrected ruler"`
and `"line-normalis"` have zero matches anywhere outside `atlas/`.

**In one sentence:** the walker's own trace format was unreadable by every
analysis tool in the directory, the only write-up describing the lane was
finished an hour before the walks produced their 88s, and the session ended
seven hours later pointing somewhere else.

## 6. The refuted claims — precise corrected statements (question 4)

The wave-1 agent established the refutations. What the 498 add here is (i) the
distinct-set histogram, (ii) the *mechanism*, (iii) a within-session smoking
gun, and (iv) a fourth claim the wave-1 pass did not reach. Corrected wordings
below are safe to publish as written.

### 6.1 The `l3 ≥ 67` floor for 88s — FALSE, and by far more than reported

> Published (`thinktank/third_frame.md` table, `NOTEBOOK.md`): the five 88s
> spread over `l3 ∈ {67, 68, 73, 74, 77}`; 67 read as the floor.

**Corrected:** *Over 504 88-gate circuits from the corrected-ruler corner sweep
(498 distinct mask sets), the socle-skeleton size `l3` runs continuously from
**64** to 79 with every integer occupied; 27 distinct 88s have `l3 ≤ 66`. Over
the 17,283-circuit e15 Run-A census the same statistic reaches **`l3 = 57`**,
with 570 circuits at `l3 ≤ 66`. There is no `l3` floor for 88 at 67; the lowest
value observed anywhere on this machine is 57, and it is attained by three
distinct circuits.*

The 67 was a **sample-size artifact of nine circuits**, not a property of the
socle plane — and the counterexamples were not only in the unread `.plat`
output, they were already in a corpus that predates the atlas session by twelve
days.

### 6.2 The empty band `l3 ∈ 69..72` — FALSE

> Published (`ideas_strange_corners.md` §4.5, `audit_inference.md` §3.3):
> *"the band `l3 ∈ 69..72` that `audit_inference.md` flagged as empty is now
> populated — and costs 90–91 gates."*

**Corrected:** *The band `l3 ∈ 69..72` is populated at **88** gates: 70 distinct
88s from the corner sweep (69:36, 70:16, 71:6, 72:12) and **7,867** from the
e15 census (69:1104, 70:2649, 71:2779, 72:1335). It is in fact the most densely
populated part of the 88 level set, not a gap in it.* The "empty band" was an
artifact of placing nine circuits on a two-dimensional plane.

### 6.3 The "real 2-gate ridge" — FALSE, and refuted by the session's own seed

> Published (`ideas_strange_corners.md` §0/§4.5, `NOTEBOOK.md` line 793–795):
> *"the two 88 bands `{67,68}` and `[73,78]` are separated by a real 2-gate
> ridge."*

**Corrected:** *There is no ridge. The 88 level set is continuous in `l3` over
`[64, 79]` in the corner sweep and over `[57, 79]` in the census, with every
integer occupied and no gap of any width.*

**The smoking gun is inside the session.** The evidence for the ridge was the
shoulder seeds `work/SH_l69_90.txt`, `SH_l70_90.txt`, `SH_l71_90.txt`,
`SH_l72_91.txt` — circuits sitting in the band at 90–91 gates. The walk launched
from `SH_l70_90.txt` itself, `out/sh.l70_a3_s1_d0.plat`, **descended to 88 gates
in that same session**, emitting seven 88-gate rows at `l3 = 64, 65, 65, 66, 66,
66, 66` (lines 35–42). The circuit used to price the band at 90 had already
disproved the pricing before the pods were terminated.

### 6.4 NEW — the socle-plane envelope's left shoulder is at 88, not 89

Not addressed by the wave-1 pass.

> Published (`ideas_strange_corners.md` §4.5 item 2): *"`89@10` has `l3 = 63` …
> So 89 gates is attainable at `l3 = 63` — sixteen below the record band — and
> the left-hand shoulder of the envelope is at **89**, not 91."*

**Corrected:** *`l3 = 63` is attainable at **88** gates — 20 census 88s sit
there — and the envelope extends to `l3 = 57` at 88 gates. The left-hand
shoulder of the socle-plane envelope is at **88**, not 89. `corner/envelope.py`
as published understates the envelope over the whole range `l3 ∈ [57, 66]`,

the `.plat` rows or the census.*

Anyone re-running `envelope.py` for publication must feed it the census and the
decoded `.plat` rows, or the plot will be wrong on its entire left half.

### 6.5 What is NOT refuted

The bimodality-by-lineage reading in `audit_inference.md` §3.3 is dead as a
statement about the plane, but the underlying observation survives in a weaker
and now-measured form: `l3` is strongly determined by `|A*|`
(`r = -0.854` over the 498, `r = -0.553` over the census) and by depth
(`r = +0.43` / `+0.60`). Lineages differ in `l3` because they differ in
cancellation apparatus, not because the plane has structure at `l3` alone.

## 7. `near87.json` — the deletion certificate

**What it is.** `wrapup/day2/atlas498/near87.json`, produced by
`tools/near87.py` (`nice -n 19`, 55.6 s, single thread). Six fields:

```json
{"pairs_overlap87": 35,
 "pairs_overlap87_all_contain_targets": true,
 "pairs_overlap87_realisable": 0,
 "deletions_tested": 27888,
 "deletions_realisable": 0,
 "pairs": [[canon_i, canon_j], … 35 entries]}
```

**The observation behind it.** Every valid 88 contains all 32 MixColumns target
masks. So if two distinct 88-gate mask sets `M_i`, `M_j` overlap in 87 masks,
the intersection is an **87-mask set that still contains all 32 targets**. The
only thing standing between such a set and an 87-gate MixColumns circuit is
whether it is *realisable* — whether every member is the XOR of two earlier
members-or-inputs. §3.5 found 35 such pairs among the 498.

Equivalently — and this is the generalisation that makes the test worth
running — `M_i ∩ M_j` is just `M_i` with one mask deleted. So the test was run
**exhaustively**: every single non-target mask of every one of the 498.

**What was computed.**

1. All 35 overlap-87 intersections: **35/35 contain the 32 targets** (asserted,
   not assumed), **0/35 realisable**.
2. All single-mask deletions: `498 × 56 = 27,888` candidate 87-mask sets — the
   count is exactly `56` per circuit, independently re-confirming `B = 56`.
   A cheap necessary condition first (after deleting `m`, every remaining mask
   must retain at least one derivation pair inside `inputs ∪ M \ {m}`):
   **10,833 of 27,888 pass it** — 39 %, so the local obstruction is far weaker
   than `B = 56` suggests. All 10,833 survivors then went through the project's
   own `corpus88.py:realisable` greedy closure, which is exact for this question
   (the closure is order-free and monotone, so it succeeds iff the set is
   realisable). **0 of 10,833 realisable.**

**Why it is stronger than what was already known.** `CORPUS88.md` closes the
deletion route with `B = 56`: every middle gate has a consumer, so no gate is
*unused*. That test does not ask whether the consumers of a deleted gate have an
**alternative derivation** inside the remaining mask set. This one does, and
allows arbitrary rewiring of the surviving 87 masks. The result is a new
permanent negative certificate:

> **No 87-gate MixColumns circuit is obtainable from any of the 498 atlas corner
> 88s by deleting one mask, even with unrestricted rewiring of the remainder.
> 27,888/27,888 closed, machine-checked.**

The 39 % pass rate on the local condition against a 0 % global pass rate also
says something structural: **the obstruction to deletion is topological
(ordering / acyclicity), not local (availability of a re-derivation).** That is
the first time the deletion barrier has been separated into those two
components.

**Confidence: VERIFIED (negative).** Machine-checked, exhaustive over the stated
population, using the project's own realisability check. It is a certificate
that closes a route, not a route to 87. Its *generalisation* is a live lead —
LEAD-1 below.

## 3. The corrected joint conclusion

With the two exact values in place the four measured points become:

```
(rank 3, no sharing)  = 4 + 8  = 12        Lane G
(rank 1, no sharing)  = 1 + 11 = 12        <-- was ">= 11"
(rank 1, sharing)     = 1 + 8  =  9
(rank 0, sharing)     = 0 + 10 = 10
```

> **The rank cut alone buys ZERO gates (12 → 12) and the `D37 → W3` sharing
> edge buys all three (12 → 9).** §3.2's "both ingredients are needed; neither
> alone accounts for 3" is **false as stated**; the honest statement is that the
> gap between lane G's derived R34 price and the record's is attributable, in
> this frame, to the sharing edge alone — and those 3 gates are free, because
> `D37` is bought anyway for `U4`.
>
> **Lane G's late solve says the same thing from the other side.** `U3(tier1) =
> 10` makes the pair `(U27, U3) = 2 + 10 = 12`, identical to the full-basis
> `4 + 8 = 12`. Lane G §4.4(b)'s *"moves from 12 towards 11"* **moves nowhere**;
> R34 does not drop from 25 to 24, and the hoped-for "first evidence that the
> greedy criterion is not optimal *within* the derived family" does not
> materialise.
>
> **Two independent instruments, on different cells, both return zero for the
> rank/basis reduction.** That is a stronger and cleaner result than the one
> either document claims — it localises the whole gap in one structural
> feature.

**No bound moves.** §3.2 pre-registered this: *"with `U4(no D37) = 11` the
bound is `B >= 12 + W3`, and the LP minimum over `{x+u >= 11, x+w >=
W3noshare, w >= 4, u >= 7}` is 19 even if this lands on 11."* It landed on 11
and the LP minimum is 19. What changes is the **attribution**, not the
arithmetic.

**The lane's own referee already caught this.** `referee/REFEREE.md`
